Frequently Asked Questions

Tap any question below to read the full answer.

1. How long does the first deposit take?
After submitting your first deposit request, please allow approximately 2–5 minutes for approval. Processing time may vary depending on payment verification. If your deposit is still pending after this time, contact Customer Support and provide your transaction reference or payment proof.
2. What should I do if my deposit is pending?
First, check your deposit history and make sure your payment was successfully completed. Do not submit the same payment repeatedly. If the request remains pending, contact Customer Support with your transaction ID and payment screenshot.
3. What are the withdrawal requirements?
According to the platform's stated rules, users must complete at least 25 tasks before becoming eligible to request a withdrawal. Please also check your available balance and any other applicable withdrawal requirements before submitting a request.
4. Why do I need to complete 25 tasks?
The platform requires users to complete 25 tasks before requesting a withdrawal. You can check your completed task count in your account. Once the required tasks are completed, check your withdrawal section for eligibility.
5. How long does a withdrawal take?
Withdrawal requests are subject to review and processing. You can check the latest status in your transaction history. If your request is taking longer than the stated processing period, contact Customer Support with your withdrawal reference.
6. What is a Lucky Order?
A Lucky Order is a special order that may appear while completing tasks. It can have a different required amount from a regular task. Always review the order details, required amount and instructions displayed on your account before proceeding.
7. What is the 24-hour Lucky Order deadline?
According to the platform's Lucky Order policy, the required outstanding amount must be completed within 24 hours after the Lucky Order is triggered. Check the order details and deadline displayed in your account. Do not make a payment unless you understand and agree to the displayed amount and conditions.
8. What happens if I miss the Lucky Order deadline?
Under the stated platform policy, if the Lucky Order is not completed within 24 hours, the applicable commission may decrease over time. The exact reduction and timing should be clearly shown in the platform's actual Lucky Order rules. Contact Customer Support if you need clarification about your order.
9. How is my commission and daily profit calculated?
Your commission is the earning credited for eligible completed tasks. Today's Profit displays the earnings recorded for the current day. You can review your account balance, today's profit and completed task count in your account dashboard.
10. How can I contact Customer Support?
You can contact our support team through the Telegram or WhatsApp buttons above. Include your account details, transaction reference and a clear description of your issue. Never share your password or verification codes with anyone.
